# Bot-Hire
AI-powered tool to detect fake jobs and recruiter scams
## Problem statement
Fake job postings are everywhere online, and they can trick job seekers into scams or misleading offers. Bot Hire is a tool that checks job postings for authenticity. It looks at job descriptions, company info, images, emails, and URLs to determine if a job is real or fake, and explains why it thinks a job might be suspicious.
